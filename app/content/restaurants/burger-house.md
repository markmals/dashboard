Burger House has served patties to Dallas-Fort Worth since 1951, and its success sure has something to do with the special seasoning used on those patties. As advertised, everything here is old-school, from the big, fat, juicy burgers to the logo. These patties are cooked on a griddle and served on sesame seed buns, with mustard as the default condiment. Add chili, cheese, or get your onions grilled. The atmosphere is best described as awash in vibrant blue and yellow, its thematic colors, and offering walk-up window ordering. You’re not here for vibes or service, you’re here to eat a damn burger.
