From the team behind numerous Texas Tex-Mex chain locations, this family-focused casual dining establishment is dishing up comfort fare, including fresh tortillas, queso, fajitas, enchiladas, and more. Do not skip the super crispy beef fajitas with fresh guacamole. Bonus: complimentary chips and salsa are available during all dining hours Monday through Sunday, with happy hour specials and family platters. There are indoor dine-in services with booth and table seating
