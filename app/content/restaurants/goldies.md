A relative newcomer on the fried chicken scene is Goldie’s in Lake Highlands. The swanky neighborhood bar and bistro recently introduced a weekly $32 per person Sunday Chicken Feast, featuring a full platter of crispy, juicy fried chicken alongside chicken nuggets, chicken salad, fries, cole slaw, pickled veggies, a King’s Hawaiian roll, a sweet chili sauce, and a horseradish honey mustard — all designed to be paired with bubbles.
