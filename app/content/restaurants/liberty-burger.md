Liberty Burger offers a family-friendly atmosphere and a crowd-pleasing menu of burgers. The classic Liberty Burger boasts an all-American combo of lettuce, tomatoes, onions, and pickles. The burgers get inventive here, from the Napa (arugula, green olives, roast tomatoes, red onion, gorgonzola, and a basil and garlic aioli) to the Baby Bella (a giant onion ring with queso and sautéed baby bella mushrooms). Our favorite is not a burger if beef is a disqualifier, but the Jackie O, a ground lamb patty with baby spinach, roasted tomatoes, feta, and tzatziki sauce. The fries are excellent, coming in skinny form or sweet potato.
