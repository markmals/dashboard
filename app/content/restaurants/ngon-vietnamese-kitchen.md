Take a trip to Vietnam via this beautiful, plant-filled cafe, named after owner Carol Nguyen’s mother. It serves Hanoi-influenced dishes like savory bun cha Hanoi (vermicelli noodles with pork belly and grilled pork patties), summer beet rolls, shaking tenderloin (a take on the famous shaking beef), and a luscious duck curry served with blue rice that gets its hue from the addition of butterfly pea flower. For dessert, try the Vietnamese egg coffee, a sweet and decadent treat that diners won’t find elsewhere in town.
