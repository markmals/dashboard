Looking for ice cream with a natural sweetness? The soft serve ice cream in this spot is sweetened with raw honey sourced from a local beekeeper and is made with local, organic Texas milk. Try its honey, butter pecan, or dark chocolate flavors, but the banana cream pie soft serve is the star of the menu. The shop sells by the cup or by the carton. There are also sundaes, shakes, and ice cream nachos.
