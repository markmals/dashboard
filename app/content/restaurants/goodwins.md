Looking for the restaurant to be at on Lower Greenville? This dressed-up bistro is it. Stop in for succulent bone-in pork chop, pan-roasted halibut, or the damn fine burger. Or simply share a plate of crispy zucchini chips and cheese beignets during happy hour. Those in the know already know that Goose Bar, tucked away in the back, is a great destination for happy hour or an after-dinner drink. Look to discover a beautifully painted mural featuring several owners’ dogs and a pegasus as an ode to the city.
