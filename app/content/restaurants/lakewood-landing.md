The Landing knows that nothing goes with a beer like a burger, and with that, it’s created a great dive bar burger. There’s nothing special hidden in the burger; it is just a solid third of a pound of quality beef cooked well, and topped with tomatoes, white onions, lettuce, and a choice of mayonnaise or mustard (you will never hear anyone here utter the word “aioli”). The aesthetic of this spot is as straightforward and no-frills as its burger. The secret to the killer burgers here is that some incredibly overqualified people sometimes cook them — Lakewood is one of the handful of popular hangouts for service industry folks in Dallas.
