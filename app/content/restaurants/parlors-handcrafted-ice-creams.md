The dream of this ice cream shop came to its husband-and-wife owners after a trip to Italy heightened their appreciation for the sweet treat. On a recent trip to this shop in East Dallas, the irresistible scent of cinnamon and sugar permeated the air as Parlor’s team was busy making waffle cones. Pair one of those warm cones fresh out of the waffle press with a big scoop of vanilla ice cream for a joyful experience. Parlor’s partners with local suppliers and Texas-based brands to craft its farm-to-scoop offerings. Try flavors like brownies a la mode, sweet cream, mint chip, and milk and cookies.
