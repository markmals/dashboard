With a name like Bubba’s, great fried chicken makes sense, but this homey, diner-style restaurant is a Dallas classic that thoroughly exceeds expectations. The #1 combo – a piece of chicken, one side, and a roll – makes for an excellent light lunch, but go ahead and splurge on the three-piece if you’re feeling like a baller.
