What has long been touted as the best burger in Dallas finally finds a permanent home in a standalone restaurant in Lake Highlands. The ample space features a full bar, giving chef Dave Culwell room to roll out a menu of just under a dozen varieties of smash burgers, along with fries, onion strings, and fried pickles. Burger Schmurger features booths and tables suitable for families, bar seats and intimate tables for casual dates, and a small, covered patio. Get the OG Schmurger if you haven’t had it yet, but the Pasadena (a nod to Culwell’s hometown) and the blue cheese burger are also highlights.
