From the team behind countless Italian-American chain locations, this family-focused casual dining establishment is dishing up comfort fare, including endless breadsticks, salads, pasta dishes, chicken parmigiana, and more. Do not skip the super garlicky breadsticks with marinara sauce. Bonus: unlimited soup, salad, and breadsticks are available during lunch Monday through Friday 11 a.m. to 4 p.m., with family-style portions and shareable entrees. There are indoor dine-in services with booth and table seating
