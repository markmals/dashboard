Desperados has served Tex-Mex on Greenville Avenue since 1976, when founder Jorge Levy and two partners opened a twelve-table dining room with recipes carried up from Mexico City. That original Uno location is still going. The menu centers on the signature Desperados Tacos — two flash-fried flour tortillas with melted jack cheese, marinated beef or chicken, pico de gallo, and sliced avocado — plus sizzling beef, chicken, or shrimp fajitas served with bean soup and guacamole. Other standouts include the brisket-filled Tacos Albanil, a poblano chile relleno stuffed with slow-roasted brisket and almonds, and bacon-wrapped gulf shrimp with queso rancho. The bar’s La Margarita has run on the same recipe since 1985.
