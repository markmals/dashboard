Sister serves Italian-ish and Mediterranean-ish fare, the “ish” being a staple of Duro Hospitality’s style: a mixture of trendy and classic. Inside, the space feels like a chic Italian grandma’s living room, if she had a thing for spritzes and took reservations. The Moroccan spiced chicken is a perennial favorite, while the pancetta carbonara takes comfort food to another level.
