This colorful spot, led by a pair of Mexican immigrants — CEO and executive chef Fino Rodríguez and his business partner Victor E. Rico — is all about Mexican cuisine. The menu travels all over the country, from Vera Cruz-style seafood-heavy dishes to Oaxaca-inspired moles to the ranches of Northern Mexico where fajitas were born. The covered patio houses an outdoor bar for festive feels. And, depending on who is in the kitchen, this place frequently doesn’t hold back on the spice.
