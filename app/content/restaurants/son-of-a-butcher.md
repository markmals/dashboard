A Lower Greenville slider bar built around mini burgers on toasted buns. The menu is anchored by the Classic — beef, comeback sauce, American cheese, and pickle — alongside signature sliders like the Popper (fried jalapeño and cream cheese, bacon, pepper jam), the Cowboy (pimento cheese, crispy onion straws), a Nashville hot fried chicken slider, and the Big Kahuna, which puts yellowfin tuna on a King’s Hawaiian ube coconut roll. Order the three-slider combo, mix the Classic with the Popper or the Smokehouse, and add onion rings and a hand-spun shake.
