The MLK beer and kolache shop offers up pillowy klobasniky filled with meats like sausage or pepperoni (including cheesy vegetarian options), and kolaches full of sweet cheeses, fruits, and nuts. Takeout orders can be placed [online](https://www.toasttab.com/catering/batch-craft-beer-kolaches) or in person; there are indoor and outdoor dine-in areas.
